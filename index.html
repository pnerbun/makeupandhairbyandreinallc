About Andreina

YOUR ARTIST

FOR EVERY MOMENT

Andreina is a Dallas-area hair and makeup artist and the founder of Makeup & Hair by Andreina LLC. She brings her full kit — and full attention — directly to you, so the morning of your event stays calm and your look stays exactly as planned.

Whether it's a wedding morning with a full bridal party, a quinceañera that deserves every detail right, or a photoshoot that needs to hold up under studio lights — Andreina builds each look from scratch for the face wearing it.
