My Story

WHO I AM

Hi, I'm Andreina — the owner and artist behind Makeup & Hair by Andreina LLC. My love for beauty started when I was a little girl. I was always fascinated by makeup, hairstyles, and the confidence that comes from feeling beautiful. What began as a childhood passion eventually became my dream career.

After becoming a mom, I realized how important it is to pursue what truly makes you happy — and that's when I decided to turn my passion into a profession. Since establishing my business in 2017, I've had the privilege of helping hundreds of women look and feel their best for some of the most important moments of their lives: weddings, quinceañeras, photoshoots, prom, maternity sessions, and special events.

My goal is not only to create stunning hair and makeup looks, but also to provide a relaxing and enjoyable experience. I love getting to know my clients, hearing their stories, and helping bring their vision to life. There is nothing more rewarding than seeing someone smile when they look in the mirror and feel like the best version of themselves.

Based in Rockwall, Texas, I offer services from my home studio and also travel to venues and locations for weddings and special events. I specialize in soft glam, full glam, bridal beauty, elegant updos, Hollywood waves, and timeless styles that photograph beautifully while still feeling like you.
